# AMI Labs

> Advanced Machine Intelligence (AMI) Labs is a frontier AI research lab building world
> models — AI systems trained on continuous, high-dimensional real-world sensor data rather
> than language, intended to understand the physical world, hold persistent memory, reason
> and plan, and remain controllable and safe. Founded by Yann LeCun (Executive Chairman) with
> Alexandre LeBrun as CEO, the lab launched publicly on 2026-03-10 with a $1.03B USD round and
> operates from Paris, New York, Montreal and Singapore.

## Developer surface

AMI Labs publishes **no public API, no documentation, no SDKs, and no machine-readable contract**
as of 2026-08-17. This is a measured absence, not a gap in our collection: amilabs.xyz is a
two-page site (its own sitemap.xml lists exactly `/` and `/updates`), every OpenAPI, GraphQL,
MCP and A2A discovery path returns HTTP 404, and no api./docs./developer./mcp. subdomain resolves
in DNS. The lab has stated it intends to open-source models and publish papers, but as of this
date it has released none — there are no models under an AMI Labs account on Hugging Face, no
first-party packages on npm or PyPI, and no GitHub organization belonging to this company.

- Note: `github.com/amilabs` and the `@amilabs/*` npm scope belong to a DIFFERENT, unrelated
  company (AmiLabs, Spain, contact@amilabs.pro, org created 2015). Do not attribute them here.
